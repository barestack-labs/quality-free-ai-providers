### How to Configure in Hermes Agent
1. Open Hermes Desktop → **Settings** → **Providers** → **Accounts**.
2. Click **Nous Portal**.
3. Authorize your account in the browser window that opens. Hermes binds your session automatically.

### Notes & Honest Gotchas
* **Card Gate**: While the plan costs $0, you must provide a card for account verification. If you do not have a card, use Kilo Gateway or b.ai instead.
* **HY3 Removal**: HY3 is no longer active on Nous Portal, use Solar Pro or LongCat 2.0 here.
